# Objection Reversal Launch

Each objection appears in quotes, gets struck out, and is flipped.

### Objection Reversal Launch

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/objection-launch · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/objection-launch/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 20s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A 20-second, all-typography launch that sells by answering doubts. After an opening line, three common objections appear in quotes, get struck through and dimmed, and are replaced by confident answers, then a closing question and the CTA.

Good fit: Products buyers are skeptical about; Typography-only launches with no screenshots.
Not for: Products that need to be seen to be understood; Calm, premium brand films.
Inputs: product, intro, objections, closer, cta, url, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the objection-launch skill for Stack, a hiring tool: objections "We don't hire often: Pay per hire, not per month | Our ATS works fine: Imports in one click | No time to learn tools: Ready in 10 minutes". Paper theme, 1:1.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 20-second 16:9 typographic launch for [who will watch, e.g. freelancers who haven't signed up], playing [where, e.g. retargeting ads and X]. Use only the objections and answers below; don't soften or change them. Near-black background.

Scene 1: the opening line rises word by word. Scenes 2–4, one per objection: a small counter "01 / 03", the objection in gray quotes gets struck through with an orange line and shrinks, and the bold white answer pops in word by word with a faint orange flash on the background. Scene 5: the closing question slams in word by word. End frame: the product name, an orange call-to-action button and the URL.

Opening line: [e.g. "You've heard it all before."]
Three objections and answers: [e.g. "Too expensive" → "Free until you send 50 invoices"]
Closing question: [e.g. "So, what's stopping you?"]
Product, call to action and URL: [e.g. Ledgerly, "Try Ledgerly free", ledgerly.io]
