# Manus-Style Feature Film

### Manus-Style Feature Film

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/manus-style-launch · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/manus-style-launch/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 25–60s
- Last checked: 2026-10-17

A launch film modelled shot by shot on the Manus 2.0 Video Editor film. It follows one real example of the product's work all the way through: a wall of finished outputs, the example floating as layers, footage organized, shots generated, then the same card in the same place gaining captions, a tracked circle, a title, a map drawn by code, photos and sound, its layers lifting apart and composing into one video, the editor, and asking the agent. Big, tight lines whose key words turn blue as they sharpen out of a blur. Give it your website: your agent captures the page, writes every line from it, and the film runs as long as your material needs.

Good fit: AI and creative tools with lots of examples to show; Feature launches with a playful, modern feel.
Not for: Products with only one screen to show; Serious, formal announcements.
Inputs: beats, pace (brisk | normal | calm), product, logo, icon, hook, wallLine, layersLine, features, tweakLine, askLine, outro, shot, stickers, screen, images, note, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use manus-style-launch to make a launch film for https://your-product.com: follow one thing it made through its main features.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 55-second 16:9 launch film for [who will watch, e.g. creators following our launch], playing [where, e.g. X and YouTube]. Use only the product name, lines and images I attach; don't change a word. Style: pale grey #f6f6f6 canvas with a faint dot grid; huge, tight, heavy sans lines at the top or bottom of the frame ending with a full stop, one or two key words in bright blue #1f9bf6, each line sharpening word by word out of a blur. Brisk, expo easing, blur transitions, nothing bounces.

Scene 1: "[Product] now makes" and a giant "[thing]." whose letters are filled with one of my images. Scene 2: a tilted wall of finished examples drifting sideways. Scenes 3–8: one example card floats in 3D while each feature acts on it in place, each with its own short line (organize, generate, caption, track, title, code, photos, sounds). Scene 9: the card's layers lift apart in 3D, then come together with a play button. Scene 10: a 2×2 grid of results, then the editor with "Edit it yourself." and "Or just ask [Product]." End frame: the logo sharpening out of a blur.

Product: [name] (e.g. Manus 2.0 Video Editor)
Feature lines: [six to eight short lines] (e.g. "Every video is made of layers.", "Circle it. It tracks.")
