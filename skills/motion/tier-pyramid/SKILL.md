### Tier Pyramid

Tiers drop in from the base up with a small bounce, each labeled inside and annotated with a dotted leader and a note on the side. The top tier lands last in the accent color, as the thing that matters most.

Good fit: Hierarchies and maturity models; Showing what matters most.
Not for: Flat lists with no ranking; More than about six tiers.
Inputs: title, tiers, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the tier-pyramid skill: title "Maslow for SaaS", tiers "Delight: wow moments | Speed: no waiting | Reliability: it works | Security: it's safe". Ink theme.
