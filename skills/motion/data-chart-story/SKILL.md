# Data Chart Story

An animated bar or line chart that lands one takeaway.

### Data Chart Story

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/data-chart-story · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/data-chart-story/install.md
- free · Data · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 9s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Bars grow with counting labels or a line draws itself point by point, the latest value is accented, and a takeaway callout lands at the end. Paste label: value pairs.

Good fit: Simple bar or line charts with one takeaway; Social posts with a number to share.
Not for: Charts with several series; Dense dashboards.
Inputs: title, data, chart (bar | line), prefix, suffix, insight, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the data-chart-story skill with our signups: "W1: 320 | W2: 410 | W3: 640 | W4: 980", title "Weekly signups", line chart, insight "3x in a month".

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 9-second 16:9 animated bar chart for [who will watch, e.g. investors], playing [where, e.g. the monthly update]. Use only the numbers I attach or list below; don't round or change any of them. Warm off-white background, bold geometric sans.

The title fades in at the top left. A baseline draws across and faint gridlines appear. The gray bars grow one after another with their values counting up above them; the last bar is orange-red with its value in the same accent. End frame: a black pill with an orange dot slides in near the last bar with the takeaway, held for 2 seconds.

Title: [e.g. "Monthly active teams"]
Data: [your labels and values] (e.g. Jan–Jun: 120, 168, 214, 290, 352, 498)
Takeaway: [e.g. "4x growth in six months"]
