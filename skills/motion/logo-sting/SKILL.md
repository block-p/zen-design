# Logo Sting & Outro

A punchy brand reveal or a clean end card.

### Logo Sting & Outro

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/logo-sting · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/logo-sting/install.md
- free · Brand · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 5s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Sting: the mark punches in with a ring burst, then the wordmark slides out from behind it. Outro: logo, name, tagline and a call-to-action button. Bring your own SVG or PNG logo.

Good fit: Intros and outros for your videos; A short brand moment with a call to action.
Not for: Complex logo animations that trace the mark; Logos you don't have as SVG or PNG.
Inputs: logo, name, tagline, cta, style (sting | outro), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the logo-sting skill with our logo at assets/logo.svg, name "Helix", tagline "Science, accelerated." Make a 16:9 sting and a 9:16 outro with the CTA "helix.bio".

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 5-second 16:9 logo sting for [who will watch, e.g. viewers of our videos], playing [where, e.g. at the end of every video]. Use only the logo, name and tagline I give below. Near-black background.

The logo mark punches in at the center with a slight rotation, an accent ring expands and fades, and twelve short rays burst outward. The mark then slides left while the wordmark in a bold geometric sans slides out from behind it. End frame: the tagline fades in below in gray and a soft light sweep passes across the lockup.
