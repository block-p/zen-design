# Quote Card

Testimonials and pull-quotes, revealed word by word.

### Quote Card

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/quote-card · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/quote-card/install.md
- free · Social · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 8s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

An editorial quote with oversized quotation marks, a word-by-word reveal and highlighter strokes on the phrases you mark with *asterisks*, then the author with avatar or initials.

Good fit: Testimonials and customer quotes; Sharing a line from a talk or article.
Not for: Long quotes; Quotes you aren't allowed to use.
Inputs: quote, author, role, avatar, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the quote-card skill for this customer quote: "Our onboarding video went from two weeks to *two hours*." — Jordan Lee, Head of Growth at Fable. Make it 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 8-second 16:9 testimonial clip for [who will watch, e.g. founders comparing tools], playing [where, e.g. our pricing page or a LinkedIn post]. Use only the quote, name and title I give below; don't change a word. Warm sand-colored background.

Scene 1: large rust-colored serif quotation marks drop in at the top left. Scene 2: the quote appears word by word in a dark editorial serif, each word rising slightly out of a blur, then a translucent rust highlighter sweeps under the key word. End frame: an avatar circle with the initials, and the name and title slide in below, held for 2 seconds.

Quote: [your customer's quote] (e.g. "We shipped our launch video in an afternoon. It looked like it came from a studio.")
Key word: [e.g. "studio"]
Name and title: [name, role, company] (e.g. Priya Nair, Founder, Lumen Labs)
