# Lyric Video

Timed lyrics with karaoke fill, kinetic words or stacking lines.

### Lyric Video

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/lyric-video · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/lyric-video/install.md
- premium · Social · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 24s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Paste timed lyric lines (LRC timestamps work) and pick a style: karaoke fill, kinetic words or stacking lines, over a backdrop that pulses at your song's BPM. Set the length to the song, render, and mux your audio in one command.

Good fit: Lyric videos for songs you have rights to; Visualizers for music releases.
Not for: Songs without timed lyrics; Music videos with footage.
Inputs: lyrics, title, artist, bpm, style (karaoke | kinetic | stack), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the lyric-video skill with lyrics.lrc for our 2:40 song at 96 BPM, kinetic style, 9:16. Mux song.mp3 at the end.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 16:9 lyric video for [who will watch, e.g. fans on YouTube], as long as the song I attach. Use only the lyrics and timestamps I give; don't change a word. Deep navy background with a violet glow that pulses at the song's tempo.

The title and artist appear first. Then each lyric line appears centered at its timestamp, words brightening from dim to full white in time with the singing. End frame: the title and artist again, held for 2 seconds.

Song and artist: [e.g. "City Light" by The Northern Rooms]
Tempo: [e.g. 112 BPM]
Attach: [the audio, and the lyrics with a timestamp per line]
