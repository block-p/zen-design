# Paper Lecture

Ruled-paper lecture notes with quizzes, drag exercises and a narrated walk-through.

### Paper Lecture

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/paper-lecture · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/paper-lecture/install.md
- free · Narrated decks · hyperframes · 16:9 · 1–3 min
- Last checked: 2026-10-12

Cream ruled paper, a red margin line and serif headings: the feel of a good set of lecture notes. Built for teaching one idea well, with a chart you can filter, a quiz, a drag-to-match and a drag-to-sort exercise, and a narration that walks through every slide.

Good fit: Teaching one idea well, with practice; University and self-paced courses.
Not for: Sales and pitch decks; Very short videos.

Example prompt: Use the paper-lecture skill to turn my lecture notes in week3.md into a narrated deck with two quizzes.

Claude Motion prompt: Turn the document I attach into a 60-second 16:9 narrated explainer for [who will watch, e.g. students], playing [where, e.g. the course page]. Use only the facts and numbers in the document. Paper lecture style.

One idea per slide, with points appearing one at a time as they're spoken. Animate one chart for the main number. End on a closing slide with one clear next step.
