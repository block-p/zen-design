# Script → Short

Turn an idea or script into a 9:16 short for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

### Script → Short

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/script-to-short · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/script-to-short/install.md
- premium · Social · hyperframes · 9:16, 1:1 · 10–20s
- Last checked: 2026-10-15

Writes or tightens the script around a hook that is on screen in the first second, splits it into beats, times every beat for reading at speed, drafts a vertical shot plan (statements, numbers, lists, quotes, comparisons), shows a storyboard, and renders an MP4 with punchy music. Works in Chinese, Japanese and Korean too.

Good fit: Faceless shorts from an idea, a post or a thread; Daily posting with one consistent look.
Not for: Talking-head videos (use Animated Captions); Videos longer than about 30 seconds.

Example prompt: Use the script-to-short skill on script.txt: a 15-second Reel, 9:16, punchy.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 15-second vertical 9:16 short for [who will watch, e.g. founders on TikTok and Reels], playing muted in the feed, so every point must read as words on screen. Use only the lines below, in this order; don't rewrite them. Black-and-white style, punchy pace.

Scene 1: the hook slams in. Scene 2: the list of points, one per beat. Scene 3: a short quote from a user. Scene 4: the proof number counts up. End frame: the closing line, held for 1.5 seconds.

Hook: [your hook] (e.g. "Stop editing videos by hand.")
Points: [3 to 4 short lines]
Quote: [a real user quote]
Proof number: [e.g. "3x more views"]
Closing line: [e.g. "Follow for the template"]
