# Transcript Highlight

A transcript plays out, the key phrase is highlighted and lifted into a pull quote.

### Transcript Highlight

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/transcript-highlight · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/transcript-highlight/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 8s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Timestamped lines appear one by one, the rest dim as a marker sweeps across the phrase you mark with *asterisks*, then the transcript steps back and the phrase rises as a big pull quote with its speaker.

Good fit: Podcast and interview highlights; Pulling a quote from a transcript.
Not for: Full subtitles; Videos without a transcript.
Inputs: lines, source, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the transcript-highlight skill with lines from our customer call: "Lena: We tried three tools. | Lena: This is the first one *my whole team actually opens*.", source "Customer call · Sept". Ink theme, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 8-second 16:9 scene for [who will watch, e.g. podcast listeners on LinkedIn], playing muted in the feed. Use only the transcript excerpt I attach or paste below; quote it word for word. Off-white background.

A white card with the show and episode name shows the transcript with gray monospace timestamps; the lines appear one by one. Then everything dims except the guest's line, and a soft orange highlighter sweeps across the key phrase. End frame: the card blurs into the background and the phrase rises as a large serif pull quote with an orange quotation mark and the speaker's name beneath.

Show and episode: [e.g. "THE BUILD PODCAST · EP. 42"]
Transcript: [the host's question and the guest's answer, with timestamps]
Key phrase and speaker: [e.g. "support tickets dropped by a third", Dana]
