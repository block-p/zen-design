The line draws steadily from start to end, and as it passes each point a dot pops and a milestone card appears, alternating above and below (stacked down the left in 9:16). The latest milestone gets the accent and a label like "Today".

Good fit: Company history and roadmaps; Project progress up to today.
Not for: More than about seven milestones; Precise dates on a scaled axis.
Inputs: title, milestones, now, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the milestone-timeline skill: title "Product history", milestones "v1: Text only | v2: Images | v3: Video | v4: Motion skills", now "Now". Ink theme, 9:16.
