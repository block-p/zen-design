# Project → Launch Video

Your agent reads the project and makes its launch video, with music.

### Project → Launch Video

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/project-to-launch · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/project-to-launch/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 15–25s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Point it at the project you're working on, or at a website. It reads the code or the live site, picks an angle and a two-second hook, rebuilds the product doing its job (the cursor types, clicks and results appear), adds the project's own claims and an end card in its brand colors, scores it with original music, and renders a 15–25 s MP4 with a poster frame and a share caption. Four tones: default, polished, chaotic, deadpan. Also works from a pull request or a release: pick a feature, fix, refactor or roll-up story.

Good fit: Launching a side project or a new app; A launch post for X, LinkedIn or Product Hunt.
Not for: Long product tours or tutorials; Projects you can't show on screen.
Inputs: product, hook, tagline, logo, flowLabel, flowInput, flowAction, flowResults, claims, punchline, cta, url, tone (default | polished | chaotic | deadpan), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the project-to-launch skill to make a 20-second launch video for this project, polished tone, 16:9.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 20-second 16:9 launch video for [who will watch, e.g. people who keep house plants], playing [where, e.g. our landing page and X]. Use only the product name, lines, screenshot and numbers I give below; don't change a word or number.

Scene 1: a two-line hook, the second line in the accent color. Scene 2: the logo and the one-line pitch. Scene 3: the app at work: a cursor types the example input, clicks the main button, and three results appear one by one. Scene 4: the proof number counts up. End frame: product name, tagline and a "Try it free" button, held for 2 seconds.

Product: [name and what it does] (e.g. Plantpal, turns a photo of a leaf into a care plan)
Hook: [two lines] (e.g. "Your plants" / "are not okay")
Example input and results: [e.g. "Monstera, yellow leaves, droopy" → three care tips]
Proof number: [e.g. "Knows 4,200 houseplants"]
Attach: [a screenshot of your app]
