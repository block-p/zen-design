A 20-second launch for products that live on several screens. Your desktop and phone screenshots rise onto a soft gradient canvas, the camera glides to the desktop, then the phone, then pulls back for both, with a glass caption per feature, before a centered lockup and CTA.

Good fit: Products that run on desktop and phone; Premium, calm launch videos.
Not for: Products with only one screen; High-energy social cuts.
Inputs: product, hook, features, image, phoneImage, cta, url, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the device-canvas-launch skill for Tally with assets/web.png and assets/app.png: hook "Expenses, handled anywhere.", three features, CTA "Start free".

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 20-second 16:9 launch video for [who will watch, e.g. ops and finance leads], playing [where, e.g. the homepage hero]. Use only the lines and screenshots I give; don't change a word. Deep navy background with soft violet and teal glows.

Scene 1: the hook rises word by word and fades. Scene 2: a browser window with the desktop screenshot and a phone with the mobile screenshot rise onto the canvas. Scene 3: the camera glides in to the browser while a glass caption shows the first feature, then to the phone with the second, then pulls back to show both with the third. End frame: the devices blur and sink as the product name, the hook and a violet call-to-action button appear.
