### Milestone Particles

A 12-second seamless loop for a real result: followers, subscribers, revenue, customers. Points drift, pull together into the number with a slow, staggered ease, an accent line draws underneath, the label settles, and everything drifts back so the last frame matches the first. Calm and premium: no confetti, no bounce.

Good fit: Follower and subscriber milestones; Revenue or customer results for a post.
Not for: Several numbers at once; Targets or rounded-up figures.
Inputs: number, label, density (low | medium | high), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the milestone-particles skill: number "48,920", label "LinkedIn followers", 9:16, my brand accent #00b37e.

End frame: the label and line fade and the points drift back to exactly where they started, so it loops.
