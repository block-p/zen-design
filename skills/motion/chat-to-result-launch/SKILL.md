# Chat-to-Result Launch

A prompt is typed, the assistant answers, and the answer becomes your launch.

### Chat-to-Result Launch

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/chat-to-result-launch · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/chat-to-result-launch/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 20s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A 20-second launch for AI-era products. A prompt types into a clean chat, the assistant replies, and its answer expands into a full-frame headline, then three feature cards and a CTA. Neutral chat styling, so it's never tied to a real app.

Good fit: AI products where a prompt produces a result; Launch videos for assistants and copilots.
Not for: Products without a chat interface; Showing your real UI step by step.
Inputs: prompt, reply, product, hook, features, cta, url, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the chat-to-result-launch skill for our product Lumen: prompt "Summarize this 40-page contract.", reply "Here are the 5 clauses that matter.", hook "Read less. Know more.", three features, CTA "Try Lumen". Ink theme, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 20-second 16:9 launch video for [who will watch, e.g. busy managers], playing [where, e.g. our homepage and LinkedIn]. Use only the product name, chat lines and features below; don't change a word. Off-white background.

Scene 1: a clean white chat window appears and the user's request types into the input, then is sent. A small orange avatar shows typing dots, then replies. Scene 2: the reply expands into a full orange frame with the product name and the headline rising word by word. Scene 3: three white feature cards follow. End frame: the product name with an orange call-to-action button and the URL, held for 2 seconds.

Product: [e.g. Orbit]
Request and reply: [e.g. "Plan my week around three deep-work blocks." → "Done. Meetings moved, focus time locked in."]
Headline: [e.g. "Your calendar, on autopilot."]
Three features: [e.g. Smart slots, Focus guard, Daily recap]
Call to action and URL: [e.g. "Try Orbit free", orbit.app]
