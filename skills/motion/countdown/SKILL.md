# Countdown

3-2-1 numbers around a draining ring, then the reveal.

### Countdown

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/countdown · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/countdown/install.md
- free · Social · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 7s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Numbers slam in with blur while a progress ring drains, then a flash reveals your final word and date. Count from 3 or 5.

Good fit: Launch teasers and event reminders; Intros before a reveal.
Not for: Countdowns longer than five; Real-time timers.
Inputs: label, from (3 | 5), final, detail, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the countdown skill for our livestream: label "Going live in", count from 5, final "We're live", detail "youtube.com/@acme". 9:16, electric theme.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 7-second 16:9 launch countdown for [who will watch, e.g. our followers], playing [where, e.g. the livestream and X the hour before launch]. Use only the date and line below. Pure black background.

Small uppercase "LAUNCHING IN" sits at the top. A thin white ring drains clockwise around each number as huge white numerals 3, 2, 1 slam in from a blurred, oversized state and shrink away. After 1, the ring blows outward and an off-white flash fills the frame. End frame: the launch line settles in with tightening letter spacing above the date and time in gray.

Launch line: [e.g. "Live now"]
Date and time: [e.g. "Oct 21 · 10am PT"]
