# Changelog Video

Release notes as a crisp, shareable clip.

### Changelog Video

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/changelog-video · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/changelog-video/install.md
- free · Developer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 10s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Product name, version badge and a bold headline, then up to five updates with New / Improved / Fixed tags that slide in and get spotlighted one by one.

Good fit: Release notes for a product update; Weekly or monthly ship posts.
Not for: Releases with more than five changes; Deep technical walkthroughs of a change.
Inputs: product, version, headline, items, footer, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the changelog-video skill for release 3.1 of Orbit: "New: Calendar sync | Improved: 40% faster load | Fixed: Export on Safari". Make a 1:1 version for social.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 10-second 16:9 release notes video for [who will watch, e.g. our users], playing [where, e.g. in-app and on X]. Use only the version and changes I give below; don't add or reword items. Deep navy background with a faint grid.

On the left, the product name with a violet version badge pops in above a big bold "What's new" headline that rises letter by letter, with the availability line beneath. On the right, four rounded cards slide in one after another, each with a small colored tag (NEW, IMPROVED, FIXED) and one line of text. End frame: each card's border lights up violet in turn.

Product and version: [e.g. Northwind v2.4]
Availability line: [e.g. "Available today · northwind.dev/changelog"]
Four changes with tags: [e.g. NEW — Brand kits]
