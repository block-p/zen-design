# Editorial Frame Launch

A magazine cover builds, then each feature is a page that turns.

### Editorial Frame Launch

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/editorial-launch · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/editorial-launch/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 20s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A 20-second launch dressed as a print magazine: masthead, issue line, cover image and an italic cover line build on a grid, then each feature arrives as a turned page with a giant numeral and pull quote, ending on a back cover with the CTA.

Good fit: Brands with an editorial or fashion feel; Launches with a strong cover image.
Not for: Developer tools and technical audiences; Fast, high-energy social cuts.
Inputs: issue, product, headline, features, image, cta, url, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the editorial-launch skill for Field Notes, a research app: issue "Vol. 3 · Spring", headline "Your notes, finally in order.", image assets/cover.jpg, three features. Sand theme.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 20-second 16:9 magazine-style launch for [who will watch, e.g. readers who like long-form writing], playing [where, e.g. our newsletter and X]. Use only the name, lines and photo I give; don't change a word. Off-white paper.

Scene 1: a large serif masthead with the issue line in small caps and a black rule, the cover photo wiping open, and the italic serif cover line. Scene 2: three pages turn in from the right, each with a running header, a giant orange serif numeral, a serif title, body text and an italic pull quote with an orange bar. End frame: the back cover with the name, a short orange rule, the italic call to action and the URL.

Name and issue line: [e.g. Margin, "ISSUE 01 · OCTOBER 2026"]
Cover line: [e.g. "The quiet return of good writing."]
Three features: [titles, one short paragraph and one pull quote each]
Call to action and URL: [e.g. "Read the first issue", margin.press]
Attach: [a cover photo]
