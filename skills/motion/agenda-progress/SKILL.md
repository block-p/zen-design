# Agenda Progress

The agenda appears, done items tick off, and a marker slides to what's next.

### Agenda Progress

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/agenda-progress · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/agenda-progress/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 6s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A numbered agenda lists out, the items before the current one dim and turn into ticks, and an accent marker slides down to the item you're moving to. Drop it between sections of a webinar, course or talk.

Good fit: Section breaks in webinars, courses and talks; Showing viewers where they are in a longer video.
Not for: Agendas longer than about seven items; Detailed schedules with times and speakers.
Inputs: title, items, current, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the agenda-progress skill: title "Workshop", items "Setup | Your first skill | Brand kit | Publishing", current 2. Ink theme, 1:1.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 6-second 16:9 agenda scene for [who will watch, e.g. webinar attendees], playing [where, e.g. between sections of the session]. Use only the title and five items below. Off-white background.

The title appears. Five numbered rows slide in. An orange highlight bar sits on the current row, the finished rows dim and their numbers turn into ticks. End frame: the bar slides down to the next item, whose text turns white and nudges right.

Title: [e.g. "Today's session"]
Five items: [e.g. "Why video wins", "Picking a format", "Writing the hook", "Rendering", "Q&A"]
Current item: [e.g. "Writing the hook"]
