# Motion Director

Describe a video; your agent directs it shot by shot and renders the MP4.

### Motion Director

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/motion-director · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/motion-director/install.md
- free · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 10–90s
- Last checked: 2026-10-15

The closest thing to Claude Motion in your own agent. It asks what the video is for, picks a proven structure (launch, explainer, data story, social short, announcement, tutorial), casts every shot from a library of 78 animated shots, shows you a storyboard before rendering, and renders an MP4 with music. Ask for changes in plain words and only the changed shots render again. 19 shots come bundled and free.

Good fit: A short animated video from an idea, notes or numbers; Launches, explainers, data stories and social shorts in one consistent look.
Not for: Editing camera footage; Videos longer than about 90 seconds.

Example prompt: Use the motion-director skill: a 20-second launch video for our 2.0 release, 16:9, from the notes in CHANGELOG.md.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 20-second 16:9 launch video for [who will watch, e.g. our existing users], playing [where, e.g. muted in the X and LinkedIn feed]. Use only the release notes and numbers I attach; don't invent features or change a number. Electric dark style, upbeat pace.

Scene 1: a bold hook line. Scene 2: the product on screen. Scene 3: the three biggest changes tick off one by one. Scene 4: two headline numbers count up. End frame: product name, one call to action and the URL, held for 2 seconds.

Attach: [your release notes or changelog]
Hook: [one line, e.g. "Release notes nobody reads? Not anymore."]
Call to action: [e.g. "Update today"]
