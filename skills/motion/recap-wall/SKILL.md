# Recap Wall

Takeaway cards fly in and settle into a tidy numbered wall.

### Recap Wall

- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 7s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Cards with your key takeaways fly in from scattered angles, land in a grid slightly askew, then straighten up as their numbers fill with the accent color. A closing line can sit beneath. Made for the end of tutorials and explainers.

Good fit: Key takeaways at the end of a tutorial; Summaries of a report or talk.
Not for: More than about six takeaways; Long explanations.
Inputs: title, points, cta, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the recap-wall skill: title "Key takeaways", points "Start small | Measure weekly | Cut what doesn't work", cta "Follow for part 2". Paper theme, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 7-second 16:9 recap scene for [who will watch, e.g. people who finished our tutorial], playing [where, e.g. the last scene of the video]. Use only the five points below. Near-black background.

A bold "Recap" title sits at the top left. Five dark rounded cards fly in one by one from random directions and angles, landing in a two-row grid slightly tilted. The cards straighten and their number circles fill orange in sequence. End frame: the closing line fades in below in gray.

Five points: [e.g. "Hook in the first second", "One idea per scene", "Captions always on", "Brand colors, not rainbow", "End with one clear ask"]
