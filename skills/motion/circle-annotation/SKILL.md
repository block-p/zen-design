# Circle Annotation

Hand-drawn circles and sticky notes mark up an image.

### Circle Annotation

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/circle-annotation · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/circle-annotation/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 8s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Your image or screenshot settles in, then marker circles loop around the spots you choose, one at a time. Each gets a tilted sticky note in the margin and a hand-drawn arrow, like a designer's review.

Good fit: Design reviews and feedback on a screenshot; Pointing out details in an image.
Not for: Video footage; Formal or corporate styles.
Inputs: image, marks, title, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the circle-annotation skill with assets/landing.png and marks "0.5, 0.15: Headline says what it does | 0.8, 0.85: One clear CTA". Sand theme, 16:9.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 8-second 16:9 annotation scene for [who will watch, e.g. investors], playing [where, e.g. the update deck]. Use only the screenshot and three notes I give. Off-white background.

The screenshot sits in the center with a soft shadow. One at a time, a rust-orange hand-drawn marker circle loops around a spot, followed by a thin black hand-drawn arrow to a white tilted sticky note in the margin with an italic serif note. End frame: all three notes showing, held for 1.5 seconds.

Attach: [a screenshot]
Three spots and notes: [e.g. revenue card → "The number investors ask about", chart spike → "Spike after the launch video", sidebar → "Everything else, one click away"]
