# Transitions Pack

Six transitions for joining slides, images or scenes.

### Transitions Pack

- premium · Transitions · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 12s

Turn 3–5 images or color cards into a captioned reel joined by your choice of transition: accent wipe, push, zoom-through, split doors, iris circle or blur dissolve. Also a reference for scene changes in your own compositions.

Good fit: Short image reels with captions; Testing scene transitions for your own videos.
Not for: Editing real footage; Long slideshows.
Inputs: slides, transition (wipe | push | zoom | split | iris | blur), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the transitions-pack skill with our event photos: "assets/1.jpg: Day one | assets/2.jpg: Workshops | assets/3.jpg: The party", transition "zoom". 9:16.

Four full-screen color cards, each with a small "01 / 04" counter and a big bold caption at the bottom left that rises in. Each card slowly zooms out. Between cards, a solid orange bar sweeps across the screen from left to right, covering the old card and revealing the next. End frame: the last card, held for 1 second.
