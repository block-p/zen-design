# Pain Reveal Launch

Everyday chaos piles up, collapses into one point, and calm is revealed.

### Pain Reveal Launch

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/pain-reveal-launch · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/pain-reveal-launch/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 20s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A 20-second launch with a comedic before/after. Little pain chips (pings, lost files, status requests) pile up and jitter, a blunt question slams in, then everything collapses into a single point and a flash reveals your calm product, three benefits and the CTA.

Good fit: Products that remove a common annoyance; Humorous before/after launches.
Not for: Serious or sensitive topics; Products without a clear pain to show.
Inputs: pains, question, product, promise, features, image, cta, url, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the pain-reveal-launch skill for Dispatch, a support inbox: pains "Tickets in 4 tools | Who replied? | Lost the thread | Angry follow-up", promise "Every customer, one inbox.", image assets/inbox.png.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 20-second 16:9 launch video for [who will watch, e.g. team leads drowning in chat], playing [where, e.g. LinkedIn ads, muted]. Use only the lines, benefits and screenshot I give; don't change a word. Off-white background.

Scene 1: white chips with red dots pop in all over the frame, tilted and jittering, each with a pain point. Scene 2: a black label slams into the center and the whole pile shakes. Scene 3: every chip is sucked into the center point, a white flash, and a calm screen appears: the product name in orange, the promise, and the screenshot rising into place. Scene 4: three benefits with orange check circles. End frame: the name with an orange call-to-action button, held for 2 seconds.

Pain points: [four short lines] (e.g. "37 unread pings", "Final_v7_REAL.pdf")
Label: [e.g. "Sound familiar?"]
Product and promise: [e.g. Calm, "One place. Zero chasing."]
Three benefits and call to action: [e.g. "Get Calm"]
Attach: [a screenshot of your product]
