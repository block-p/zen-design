# Soft Workshop

### Soft Workshop

A peach-to-lilac wash, rounded white cards with soft shadows, and violet and coral accents. Built for team workshops, trainings and onboarding sessions: a simple model, good and bad examples side by side, and practice the audience can do on the spot.

Good fit: Team workshops, trainings and onboarding; Good and bad examples with practice.
Not for: Investor or board decks; Highly technical material.

Example prompt: Use soft-workshop to make a 45-minute onboarding session on our code review guidelines.

Claude Motion prompt: Turn the document I attach into a 60-second 16:9 narrated explainer for [who will watch, e.g. managers], playing [where, e.g. a training session]. Use only the facts and numbers in the document. Soft workshop style.

One idea per slide, with points appearing one at a time as they're spoken. Animate one chart for the main number. End on a closing slide with one clear next step.
