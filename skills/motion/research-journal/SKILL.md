# Research Journal

A kraft-paper research readout with sticky-note quotes and theme charts.

### Research Journal

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/research-journal · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/research-journal/install.md
- premium · Narrated decks · hyperframes · 16:9 · 1–3 min
- Last checked: 2026-10-12

Kraft paper, olive and rust ink, taped sticky-note quotes and stamped labels. Built for user research, interview readouts and survey results: who you talked to, the themes that came up, the quote that sums it up, and what to do next.

Good fit: User research and interview readouts; Survey results with real quotes.
Not for: Financial reporting; Product launches.

Example prompt: Use research-journal to turn interview-notes/ into a 7-slide readout with the top themes and quotes.

Claude Motion prompt: Turn the document I attach into a 60-second 16:9 narrated explainer for [who will watch, e.g. the product team], playing [where, e.g. the research readout]. Use only the facts and numbers in the document. Research journal style.

One idea per slide, with points appearing one at a time as they're spoken. Animate one chart for the main number. End on a closing slide with one clear next step.

Topic: [your topic] (e.g. customer interviews)
Attach: [your document, notes or data]
