# Blueprint Launch

Your product as a technical drawing that draws itself, then fills in solid.

### Blueprint Launch

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/blueprint-launch · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/blueprint-launch/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 20s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A 20-second launch on blueprint blue. An app window draws itself in white lines with dimension marks and a drawing-sheet title block, each feature is called out as "DETAIL A/B/C" with a yellow ring, then the drawing fills in with your real screenshot and gets an APPROVED stamp.

Good fit: Developer tools and technical products; Launches that call out three specific features.
Not for: Consumer or lifestyle brands; Products without a desktop screenshot.
Inputs: product, hook, features, cta, url, image, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the blueprint-launch skill for Torque, a dev tool: hook "Built like infrastructure.", features "Retries: Automatic, exponential | Tracing: Every hop, recorded | Scale: Millions of jobs a day", image assets/dashboard.png.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 20-second 16:9 launch video for [who will watch, e.g. engineers and product teams], playing [where, e.g. Product Hunt and X]. Use only the name, lines and screenshot I give; don't change a word. Deep blueprint-blue background with a fine white grid.

Scene 1: a wireframe of an app window draws itself in thin white lines (header, sidebar, three cards, a chart) with dashed dimension lines labeled in millimeters and a drawing title block in the corner with the project name. Scene 2: the product name scrambles in, monospace, with the tagline in yellow. Scene 3: three yellow rings circle details labeled DETAIL A, B, C with feature names. Scene 4: the wireframe fills in with the screenshot from left to right and a yellow "APPROVED" stamp slams on. End frame: the name, a yellow status tag and the URL.

Product and tagline: [e.g. Orbit, "Engineered for focus."]
Three features: [names]
Status tag and URL: [e.g. "Now in public beta", orbit.app]
Attach: [a screenshot of your product]
