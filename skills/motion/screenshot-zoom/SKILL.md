### Screenshot Zoom Callout

Your screenshot settles into a clean browser frame, the camera pushes smoothly into the area you choose, everything around it dims, and a pill callout explains what the viewer is looking at.

Good fit: Pointing at one detail in a screenshot; Release notes and support answers.
Not for: Several areas at once (use SaaS UI Walkthrough); Mobile screenshots (use Phone Callouts).
Inputs: image, url, focus, label, zoom (soft | medium | strong), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the screenshot-zoom skill with our screenshot assets/billing.png, url "app.ledgerly.io/billing", focus "0.05, 0.6, 0.4, 0.25", label "Invoices send themselves". Strong zoom, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 7-second 16:9 product scene for [who will watch, e.g. prospects], playing [where, e.g. the feature page]. Use only the screenshot and caption I give. Off-white background.

A browser window with the screenshot settles in with a soft shadow. After a beat, the camera pushes smoothly into the detail I name. The rest of the screenshot dims under a dark overlay while the detail gets an orange outline, and an orange pill caption pops in just below it. End frame: the camera keeps drifting in very slightly.
