### Chapter Stamp

The chapter number slams down from oversized with a small camera shake, an accent rule extends, the label tracks in, and the chapter title rises word by word as the number turns the accent color.

Good fit: Chapter openers in tutorials and long videos; Energetic section breaks.
Not for: Calm or minimal styles; Videos with only one section.
Inputs: label, number, title, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the chapter-stamp skill: label "Part", number "III", title "Rendering at scale". Paper theme, grotesk font, 9:16.
