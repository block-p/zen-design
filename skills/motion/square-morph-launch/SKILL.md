An 18-second launch built from a single shape. One accent square pops in, flips, stretches into an underline for the hook, becomes a circle, a card and a phone for each feature, and finally turns into the logo mark beside your name.

Good fit: Minimal brands with a simple geometric mark; Launches built around one strong idea.
Not for: Products that need screenshots to be understood; Busy, colorful brands.
Inputs: product, hook, features, cta, url, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the square-morph-launch skill for Grid, a layout tool: hook "Layouts that fix themselves.", three features, CTA "Try Grid". Electric theme, 1:1.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 18-second 16:9 minimal launch for [who will watch, e.g. design system teams], playing [where, e.g. the homepage hero]. Use only the name, lines and features below; don't change a word. Off-white background, one orange shape.

End frame: it shrinks into a small rotated square logo mark beside the name, with a black call-to-action button and the URL below.
