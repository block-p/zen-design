# Statement Burst

A short statement slams in word by word, then the key word bursts.

### Statement Burst

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/statement-burst · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/statement-burst/install.md
- free · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 6s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Each word punches in from a blur at a punchy or calm pace. The word you mark with *asterisks* turns the accent color while rays burst out around the statement. Built for hooks, thesis lines and section openers.

Good fit: Hooks, thesis lines and section openers; Bold one-line statements.
Not for: More than one sentence; Quiet, understated videos.
Inputs: statement, kicker, pace (punchy | calm), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the statement-burst skill: "Nobody reads. *Everybody* watches." Calm pace, paper theme, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 6-second 16:9 kinetic statement for [who will watch, e.g. followers on LinkedIn], playing muted in the feed. Use only the sentence below; don't change a word. Near-black background, bold geometric sans, centered.

The words punch in one at a time from a slight blur and larger scale. When the last word lands, the key word flips to orange and short orange rays burst outward around the whole statement and retract. End frame: the text slowly scales up by a few percent and holds.
