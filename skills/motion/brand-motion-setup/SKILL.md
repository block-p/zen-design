Set up your brand once; every skill after it renders in your colors, type and motion.

A short two-part interview plus 3–5 reference frames become three files in your project: brand.md (who you are, what you promote, your assets), MOTION.md (colors, type, timing, how things move, five things never to do, with ASK ME wherever the frames don't say), and brand.json, the colors and fonts every Motion Skills template reads. A check script flags text or accents that won't read on your background and suggests a nearby color, and a read-first rule tells your agent to use all of it, every time.

Good fit: Starting motion work for a brand; Making every skill's output look like yours.
Not for: Designing a new brand from scratch; One-off videos with a different look.

Example prompt: Use the brand-motion-setup skill to set up my brand. My site is fernleaf.studio and my reference frames are in examples/.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a [length, e.g. 15-second] 16:9 [what, e.g. product teaser] for [who will watch], playing [where]. Use only the colors, fonts, logo and timing in the brand guide I attach; ask me about anything it doesn't cover. The guide sets the look, not the ambition: go all out within it.

End frame: the logo and [call to action], held for 2 seconds.

Attach: [your brand guide, logo and any motion rules]
