# Data → Video

Turn a spreadsheet or CSV into a short data video with the story found for you.

### Data → Video

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/data-to-video · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/data-to-video/install.md
- premium · Data · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 20–40s
- Last checked: 2026-10-15

Profiles your table (time series, funnel, breakdown, before and after), finds what is worth saying (the headline number, the biggest change, the biggest leak), drafts a shot plan from the real numbers, shows a storyboard, and renders an MP4 with counting numbers, animated charts and music. Every number on screen comes from your file, and the data never leaves your machine.

Good fit: Monthly or quarterly results, milestones and dashboards as a short video; Turning one chart into a story people remember.
Not for: Live dashboards that update by themselves; Tables with dozens of series in one chart.

Example prompt: Use the data-to-video skill on revenue.csv: a 25-second results video for the team, 16:9.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 25-second 16:9 data video for [who will watch, e.g. investors], playing [where, e.g. the monthly update email]. Use only the numbers in the file I attach; don't round or change any of them. Dark ink style.

Scene 1: the headline number counts up. Scene 2: a line chart across the period draws in, and the biggest jump is marked with a label. Scene 3: one takeaway sentence. End frame: the source of the data and our name, held for 2 seconds.

Attach: [your CSV or spreadsheet] (e.g. monthly revenue, January to June)
Takeaway: [one sentence, or ask Claude to suggest one from the data]
