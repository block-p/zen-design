# Checklist Ticks

A checklist where every box ticks itself, one by one.

### Checklist Ticks

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/checklist-ticks · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/checklist-ticks/install.md
- free · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 7s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Rows slide in as cards, then each checkbox fills with the accent color and draws its tick while the item dims as done. An optional badge confirms the whole list is complete.

Good fit: Requirements, setup steps and launch checklists; Showing that everything is done.
Not for: Lists longer than about seven items; Steps that need detailed explanation.
Inputs: title, items, done, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the checklist-ticks skill: title "Launch day", items "Docs live | Pricing page | Changelog | Tweet thread | Demo video", done badge "All set". Electric theme, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 7-second 16:9 checklist for [who will watch, e.g. our content team], playing [where, e.g. the training page]. Use only the title and four items below; don't reword them. Warm off-white background.

The title fades up, then four white rounded cards slide in from the left, each with an empty checkbox and a label. One by one each checkbox fills rust orange with a little bounce, a white tick draws in, and its label dims. End frame: a small orange pill pops in below, held for 1.5 seconds.

Title: [e.g. "Before you hit publish"]
Four items: [e.g. "Hook in the first second", "Captions on", "Logo at the end", "Exported in 9:16 too"]
Pill: [e.g. "Ready to ship"]
