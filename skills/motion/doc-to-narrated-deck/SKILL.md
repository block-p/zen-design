# Doc → Narrated Deck

Hand your agent a document; get a narrated, interactive deck and an MP4.

### Doc → Narrated Deck

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/doc-to-narrated-deck · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/doc-to-narrated-deck/install.md
- premium · Narrated decks · hyperframes · 16:9 · 1–3 min
- Last checked: 2026-10-12

The director for narrated decks. Your agent reads the document, plans one idea per slide, picks a matching deck style, writes the slides and the speaker script, checks every slide, records a local narration and exports a video. Works with any installed deck style, or the built-in Plain theme.

Good fit: Turning a document into a narrated video deck; Courses, briefings and internal updates.
Not for: Videos under a minute; Footage-based or cinematic videos.

Example prompt: Use doc-to-narrated-deck on onboarding-guide.pdf: make a narrated training deck with a quiz every few slides, and export an MP4.

Claude Motion prompt: Turn the attached document into a 90-second 16:9 narrated explainer for [who will watch, e.g. new hires], playing [where, e.g. the onboarding portal]. Use only the facts and numbers in the document.

One idea per scene, with the key points appearing as they're spoken. Animate one chart for the main number. End on a closing scene with one clear next step.

Attach: [your document]
Next step: [e.g. "Book your first 1:1 with your manager"]
