# Social Post Card

A post that types itself out and racks up likes.

### Social Post Card

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/social-post-card · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/social-post-card/install.md
- free · Social · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 8s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A clean, unbranded social post: avatar, verified badge, the text typing out with a caret, an optional image, then reply / repost / like counters that count up and a like that pops.

Good fit: Sharing a post or announcement as video; Social proof in launch videos.
Not for: Recreating a specific platform's branded look; Long threads.
Inputs: name, handle, avatar, text, image, likes, reposts, replies, time, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the social-post-card skill with this customer post: name "Rina K.", handle "rinabuilds", text "Switched our docs to Folio and support tickets dropped 30% in a week." Likes 3.4k. 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 8-second 16:9 social post animation for [who will watch, e.g. prospects on LinkedIn], playing [where, e.g. a launch thread]. Use only the post, name and counts below; don't change a word or number. A generic social card, not any real platform's design. Dark background with drifting violet and teal glows.

The card rises with a slight 3D tilt. A violet avatar with initials, the name with a check badge popping in, and the handle. The post text types out character by character with a violet caret. End frame: the timestamp fades in and the reply, repost and like counters count up, with the heart turning pink and pulsing.

Name, initials and handle: [e.g. Ada Park, AP, @adapark]
Post: [the real post] (e.g. "Just rendered our whole launch video from a single prompt...")
Counts: [replies, reposts, likes] (e.g. 318, 2.1k, 12.4k)
