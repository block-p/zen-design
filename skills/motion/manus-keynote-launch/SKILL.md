# Manus Keynote Launch

A chaptered launch: a black intro, a ticker of what's new, chapters of the product at work over landscapes, One More Thing, mascots.

### Manus Keynote Launch

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/manus-keynote-launch · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/manus-keynote-launch/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 40–120s
- Last checked: 2026-10-17

A launch film modelled shot by shot on the "Introducing Manus 2.0" film: a big release told in chapters. A black intro with the icon and the version, one huge word, a ticker rolling through what's new, then a chapter card (a small icon and a name) before each capability: the product at work in a window or a phone, over full-frame landscapes with frosted glass cards, montages of what it made. Then One More Thing, a crowd of mascots, and the logo among them. Quiet, small type with one or two words in blue. Give it your website: your agent captures the page, writes every line from it, and the film runs as long as your material needs.

Good fit: Big releases with several new capabilities; AI products, platforms and apps with real screens to show.
Not for: One feature in depth (use manus-style-launch); Products with no screens to show.
Inputs: beats, pace (brisk | normal | calm), product, version, headline, tagline, icon, logo, note, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use manus-keynote-launch to make a launch film for our 2.0 release at https://your-product.com: one chapter per new capability.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 90-second 16:9 launch keynote for [who will watch, e.g. our users and press], playing [where, e.g. the launch livestream and YouTube]. Use only the product names, chapter lines and images I attach; don't change a word. Style: pale grey #f5f5f5 ground, quiet medium-weight sans lines in dark grey with one or two words in blue #2a8af6, each line fading in word by word out of a slight blur. Calm, expo easing, soft blur cuts.

Scene 1: black, a small icon and the version, then "Introducing [Product]". Scene 2: a huge "See", then "See what's new". Scene 3: a black ticker with a blue "New" beside a column of words rolling through the product areas. Scenes 4–8: one chapter per feature, each opened by a card with a small blue line icon and its name, then the product at work. Scene 9: "One More Thing" and the surprise. End frame: the logo in the middle, held for 2 seconds.

Product and version: [e.g. Manus 2.0]
Chapters: [four to six feature names, each with one line]
One more thing: [your surprise]
Attach: [screenshots and stills for each chapter]
