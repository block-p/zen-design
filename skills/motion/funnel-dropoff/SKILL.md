# Funnel Drop-off

Funnel stages narrow down, and the worst leak gets flagged.

### Funnel Drop-off

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/funnel-dropoff · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/funnel-dropoff/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 8s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Stage bars grow centered and narrow down the funnel with their counts, the drop-off percentage appears between each pair, and the biggest leak lights up in the accent color while the other stages fade.

Good fit: Conversion and signup funnels; Finding where users drop off.
Not for: Data that doesn't narrow step by step; More than about six stages.
Inputs: title, stages, focus (biggest | none), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the funnel-dropoff skill: title "Checkout", stages "Cart: 5200 | Shipping: 3100 | Payment: 1400 | Done: 1250". Paper theme, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 8-second 16:9 funnel for [who will watch, e.g. the growth team], playing [where, e.g. the weekly metrics review]. Use only the numbers I attach or list below; the drop percentages must match them. Near-black background.

The title appears. Five centered off-white bars grow one under another, each narrower, with labels and counts on the right. Small gray pills between them show the drop. End frame: the biggest drop's pill turns orange and grows, and the bars not involved fade.
