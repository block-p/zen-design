# Slides to Video

A deck rendered as a video: one slide per beat, points reveal in order.

### Slides to Video

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/slides-to-video · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/slides-to-video/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 20s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Write slides as Title: point; point; point and get a clean MP4 deck with your choice of slide, fade or zoom transitions, optional images per slide, a progress bar and footer. Ideal for all-hands updates, course modules and async reports.

Good fit: Simple slides turned into an MP4 quickly; Async updates and course modules.
Not for: Narration and interactive slides (use the narrated decks); Highly designed presentations.
Inputs: slides, images, seconds, transition (slide | fade | zoom), footer, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the slides-to-video skill: 6 slides from my outline in notes.md, 5 seconds each, fade transition, ink theme.

Claude Motion prompt: Turn the slides I attach into a 20-second 16:9 video for [who will watch, e.g. people who missed the all-hands], playing [where, e.g. Slack]. Use only the text on the slides; don't add or reword points. Off-white background.

Each slide gets a bold title and bullet points with orange dots appearing one by one. Slides slide in from the right, and a thin orange progress bar grows along the bottom. End frame: the last slide, held for 2 seconds, with the footer showing throughout.

Attach: [your slides] (e.g. a five-slide Q3 update)
Footer: [e.g. "Acme · All-hands"]
