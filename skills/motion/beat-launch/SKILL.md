A 16-second high-energy launch cut to a fixed tempo you set in BPM, so it lines up with your music. Single words flash one per beat across alternating color cards, the product name drops and pulses, three benefits hit on the beat, and the CTA pulses until the end.

Good fit: High-energy launches cut to music; Teasers for social feeds.
Not for: Calm or premium brands; Explaining how a product works in detail.
Inputs: words, product, features, cta, bpm, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the beat-launch skill at 128 BPM: words "Shoot | Cut | Grade | Post", product "Reel", features "Auto captions | Auto reframe | Auto music", cta "reel.app". Electric theme, 9:16.
