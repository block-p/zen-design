White paper, navy ink and a ruled memo header. Built for quarterly reviews, board updates and post-mortems: numbers first, a chart that compares periods, the options side by side, and an ask on the last slide.

Good fit: Quarterly reviews, board updates and post-mortems; Decks that end with a decision to make.
Not for: Playful or consumer-facing presentations; Teaching beginners.

Example prompt: Use boardroom-memo to turn q3-numbers.csv and my notes into a 6-slide board update ending with a decision.
