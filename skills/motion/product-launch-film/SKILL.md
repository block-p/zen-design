# Product Launch Film

Hook, product reveal, three features, end card.

### Product Launch Film

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/product-launch-film · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/product-launch-film/install.md
- premium · Launch · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 18s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

An 18-second launch film in four acts: a two-line hook, your screenshot rising in a browser frame with 3D tilt, three numbered features with a progress bar, and a logo end card with CTA. Accent wipes stitch the scenes.

Good fit: Web product launches with a screenshot; Landing-page hero videos.
Not for: Mobile-only apps; Very short social teasers.
Inputs: product, hook, tagline, screenshot, features, logo, cta, url, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the product-launch-film skill for Orbit, our calendar app: hook "Meetings | that run themselves", tagline "Scheduling that respects your focus.", screenshot assets/app.png, features "Smart slots: Finds time without the back-and-forth. | Focus guard: Protects deep work. | Recaps: Notes and actions, automatically.", CTA "Try Orbit", url orbit.app.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 18-second 16:9 product launch film for [who will watch, e.g. product managers], playing [where, e.g. our homepage hero and X]. Use only the name, lines and screenshot I give below; don't change a word. Near-black background with a warm orange glow.

Scene 1 (0–3.4s): a two-line hook rises from masks, line one off-white, line two orange, slowly pushing in. An orange wipe cuts to Scene 2: the product name and tagline on the left while a browser window with the screenshot rises from below with a 3D tilt and settles. Scene 3: three features one at a time, each with a huge orange numeral (01, 02, 03), a bold title and a gray description, with a segmented progress bar at the bottom. End frame: the logo mark spins in, then the name, an orange call-to-action pill and the URL.

Hook: [two lines] (e.g. "Your roadmap" / "finally in motion")
Product and tagline: [e.g. Northwind, one line]
Features: [three titles with one-line descriptions]
Call to action and URL: [e.g. "Start free", northwind.dev]
Attach: [a screenshot of your product]
