# Explainer Director

Turn an article, notes or just a topic into an explainer video.

### Explainer Director

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/explainer-director · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/explainer-director/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 30–90s
- Last checked: 2026-10-10

Writes or tightens the script with proven presets (myth → truth, how it works, listicle, number story), fact-checks every claim against your material, casts each beat to an animated shot in one shared look, shows a storyboard for approval, and renders an MP4 with music or your voiceover. 12 shots come bundled; edits re-render only the beats you change.

Good fit: 30–90 second explainers from a script or voiceover; Combining many scenes in one consistent look.
Not for: Live footage editing; Videos longer than a few minutes.

Example prompt: Use the explainer-director skill on notes.md: a 45-second explainer, how-it-works preset, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 30-second 16:9 explainer for [who will watch, e.g. our product and support teams], playing [where, e.g. the all-hands]. Use only the points and numbers in what I attach; don't add claims. One warm paper style throughout.

Scene 1: a bold hook statement. Scene 2: a funnel that shows where people drop off. Scene 3: a three-step fix, one step at a time. End frame: a recap wall of the key points, held for 2 seconds.

Topic: [your topic] (e.g. why docs lose customers)
Attach: [your notes, doc or data]
