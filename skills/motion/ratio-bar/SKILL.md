# Ratio Bar

One bar splits into shares, then the part that matters gets called out.

### Ratio Bar

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/ratio-bar · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/ratio-bar/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 7s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A single rounded bar wipes in and divides into proportional segments whose percentages count up, with a legend beneath. Then the segment you name grows, the rest of the legend fades, and a callout line explains it.

Good fit: Showing a split or a budget breakdown; Highlighting one share of the total.
Not for: Many small segments; Changes over time.
Inputs: title, parts, focus, note, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the ratio-bar skill: title "Our traffic", parts "Search: 52 | Social: 31 | Referral: 17", focus "Social", note "Up 3x since we started posting video." Ink theme.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 7-second 16:9 scene for [who will watch, e.g. the video team], playing [where, e.g. the monthly retro]. Use only the three shares below; they must add up to 100%. Off-white background.
