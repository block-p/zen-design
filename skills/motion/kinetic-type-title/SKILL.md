A title card with taste: kicker, two-line headline with an accented line, and a supporting line. Choose a masked rise, a letter cascade or a scale slam. Text auto-fits 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1, and Chinese, Japanese and Korean headlines work out of the box.

Good fit: Title cards, intros and section openers; Headlines in Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
Not for: Long paragraphs; Videos that need images or footage.
Inputs: kicker, headline, subline, accentLine (first | last | none), style (rise | cascade | slam), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).
