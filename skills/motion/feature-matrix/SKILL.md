# Feature Matrix

A comparison table fills row by row, with your column highlighted.

### Feature Matrix

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/feature-matrix · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/feature-matrix/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 8s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Your column is outlined in the accent color, then each row slides in with its label while ticks, dashes or values pop into every column. Text values such as prices work too.

Good fit: Comparing plans or competitors; Pricing and feature tables.
Not for: More than about six rows; Comparisons without a clear winner.
Inputs: columns, rows, title, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the feature-matrix skill: columns "Pro | Free", rows "Projects: Unlimited,3 | Custom domain: y,n | Analytics: y,n | Support: 24h,Community". Paper theme, 1:1.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 8-second 16:9 comparison table for [who will watch, e.g. buyers comparing tools], playing [where, e.g. the comparison page]. Use only the products, rows and values below; only claim what's true today. Near-black background.

Three column headers appear, and our column is outlined in orange with a faint orange fill that grows from top to bottom. Row by row, labels slide in from the left while ticks, gray dashes and prices pop into each column; the ticks in our column are orange. End frame: the full table, held for 2 seconds.

Columns: [e.g. "Us", "Tool A", "Tool B"]
Rows and values: [e.g. "9:16 and 1:1 export", "Brand colors", "Chinese text", "Self-review loop", "Price"]
