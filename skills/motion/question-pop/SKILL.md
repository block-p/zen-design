# Question Pop

The audience's question pops up in a speech bubble, then the answer.

### Question Pop

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/question-pop · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/question-pop/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 7s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A speech bubble springs in with the question typed word by word, a round question-mark badge bounces on its corner, and then the bubble steps back as your short answer rises in the accent color.

Good fit: FAQ clips and quiz moments; Hooks that open with a question.
Not for: Long answers; Formal reports.
Inputs: asker, question, answer, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the question-pop skill: asker "Founders ask", question "When should we hire our first designer?", answer "Earlier than you think." Sand theme, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 7-second 16:9 scene for [who will watch, e.g. our followers], playing [where, e.g. the start of a FAQ video]. Use only the label, question and answer line below. Deep navy background.

A small gray uppercase label appears, then a dark rounded speech bubble with a tail springs in from its lower left. Inside, the question appears word by word in white. A violet circular badge with a question mark pops onto the bubble's top-right corner and wobbles. End frame: the bubble shrinks slightly and dims while the answer line rises below in violet.
