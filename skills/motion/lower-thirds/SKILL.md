### Lower Thirds

- free · Captions · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 6s

Card, broadcast blocks, minimal underline or pill with initials — positioned left, center or right. Render over a demo backdrop, or as a transparent MOV overlay for your editor.

Good fit: Names and titles over interviews and talks; Transparent overlays for your video editor.
Not for: Full subtitles; Long text.
Inputs: name, role, style (bar | block | underline | pill), position (left | center | right), backdrop (demo | none), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the lower-thirds skill to make a transparent overlay for our interview: name "Dr. Lena Ortiz", role "Chief Scientist, Helix", pill style, bottom left. Render it as a MOV with transparency.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 6-second 16:9 lower third for [who will watch, e.g. viewers of our interview video], playing [where, e.g. over the speaker's first appearance]. Use only the name and title below. Over a softly blurred, warm-toned video backdrop, or over the clip I attach.
