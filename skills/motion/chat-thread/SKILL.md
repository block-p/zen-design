# Chat Thread

A conversation plays out with typing dots and popping bubbles.

### Chat Thread

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/chat-thread · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/chat-thread/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 8s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Messages appear one by one in a clean chat window: a typing indicator bounces, then each bubble pops in on its side. The second person appears on the right in the accent color. Works with any names, so it's never tied to a real app.

Good fit: Customer stories and support examples; Showing a conversation between two people.
Not for: Long conversations; Recreating a real messaging app's look.
Inputs: messages, header, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the chat-thread skill: messages "Customer: Is there a free plan? | Support: Yes, forever free up to 3 projects. | Customer: Sold.", header "Support". Electric theme, 9:16.

Claude Motion prompt: Make an 8-second 16:9 chat scene for [who will watch, e.g. teams who'd use our product], playing [where, e.g. X and LinkedIn]. Use only the channel name and messages below, in order. A generic chat window, not any real app's design. Off-white background.

A white rounded window with the channel header fades up. Messages play out one by one, each preceded by a gray bubble with three bouncing typing dots: the other person on the left in gray bubbles, the replies on the right in orange bubbles. End frame: the full thread, held for 1.5 seconds.

Channel: [e.g. "#launch"]
Messages: [name and line, 4 messages] (e.g. Maya: "Can we get a launch video by Friday?" / Reply: "Already rendering it 🎬")
