# 30s Report Explainer

A quarterly report as a 30-second editorial explainer.

### 30s Report Explainer

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/report-explainer · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/report-explainer/install.md
- premium · Data · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 30s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Five acts in an editorial serif style: title card, three KPIs that count up, a monthly bar chart, numbered takeaways and a closing line. Built for all-hands, investor updates and board decks.

Good fit: All-hands, investor and board updates; Monthly or quarterly results in 30 seconds.
Not for: Interactive dashboards; Reports with many charts.
Inputs: org, period, title, kpis, chartTitle, data, takeaways, closing, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the report-explainer skill for our Q4 all-hands: org "Acme", period "Q4 2026", title "The year we | went global", kpis "$18M: ARR | 42: Countries | 71: NPS", data from assets/revenue.csv (convert to label: value), takeaways and closing from the notes below.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 30-second 16:9 quarterly report explainer for [who will watch, e.g. the all-hands], playing [where, e.g. on the big screen]. Use only the numbers in the report I attach; don't round or change any of them. Off-white paper, editorial serif.

Scene 1: the company and quarter in small caps, then a two-line headline rises line by line with the second line in red-orange italic, and a black rule draws across. Scene 2: three KPIs with black top rules count up. Scene 3: a monthly bar chart grows with values on top and the last bar in orange-red. Scene 4: three numbered takeaways slide in. End frame: the next step, held for 2 seconds.

Attach: [your quarterly report]
Headline: [e.g. "A quarter of compounding growth"]
Next step: [e.g. "Next: expand to EMEA in Q4."]
