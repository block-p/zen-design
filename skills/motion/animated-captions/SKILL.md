A verbatim subtitle rail plus one cinematic peak word composited behind the speaker. Picks from a shortlist of proven caption styles per use case, refuses clips that would look bad, and checks readability before rendering. Runs locally: transcription and matting included, no API keys.

Good fit: Talking-head clips for Reels, Shorts and TikTok; Podcast and interview highlights.
Not for: Clips without clear speech; Full-length videos that need a professional subtitle file.
