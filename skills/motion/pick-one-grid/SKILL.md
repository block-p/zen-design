# Pick-One Grid

A grid of options; a selection ring hops around and lands on the winner.

### Pick-One Grid

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/pick-one-grid · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/pick-one-grid/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 7s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

Options pop into a grid, then a ring hops between a few candidates before landing on the one you marked with *asterisks*. The rest dim, the winner fills with the accent color and gets a label.

Good fit: Revealing a choice or a winner; Quizzes and recommendations.
Not for: Comparing options in detail; More than about nine options.
Inputs: title, options, pickLabel, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the pick-one-grid skill: title "Our stack", options "Postgres | *SQLite* | MySQL | Mongo", pick label "We chose". Paper theme, 1:1.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 7-second 16:9 scene for [who will watch, e.g. founders choosing a channel], playing [where, e.g. a LinkedIn carousel or talk]. Use only the question, six options and the pick below. Deep navy background.

The question appears at the top. Six dark rounded cards pop into a 3-by-2 grid. A violet outline ring appears on one card and hops quickly to two others before landing on the pick. End frame: the other cards dim and shrink slightly, the pick fills violet and grows, and a small pill appears above it.

Question: [e.g. "Which channel first?"]
Six options: [e.g. Blog, Newsletter, Short video, Podcast, Webinars, Ads]
Pick and pill: [e.g. "Short video", "Start here"]
