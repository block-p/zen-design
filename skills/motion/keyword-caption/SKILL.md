# Keyword Caption

A designed caption whose keywords get a pill, underline or color.

### Keyword Caption

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/keyword-caption · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/keyword-caption/install.md
- premium · Explainer · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 6s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

One sentence rises in word by word, then the keywords you mark with *asterisks* get a filled pill, a thick underline or the accent color. Place it in the center as a title card or in the lower third over footage.

Good fit: Title cards and lower-third captions; Emphasizing key words in one sentence.
Not for: Full subtitles for a whole video; Paragraphs of text.
Inputs: line, position (center | lower), mark (pill | underline | color), theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the keyword-caption skill: "Teams that *document decisions* ship *30% faster*." Underline style, lower third, ink theme.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 6-second 16:9 caption for [who will watch, e.g. marketers on LinkedIn], playing muted in the feed. Use only the sentence below; don't change a word. Warm off-white background, bold sans, centered.

The sentence rises word by word. Then a rounded rust-orange pill wipes in from left to right behind the key phrase and those words turn white. End frame: hold, 2 seconds.

Sentence: [e.g. "Short videos get twice the reach of static posts."]
Key phrase: [e.g. "twice the reach"]
