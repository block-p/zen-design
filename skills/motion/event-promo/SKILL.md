# Event Promo

A poster-style event announcement with a speaker ticker.

### Event Promo

- Page: https://claude-motion.com/skills/event-promo · Install: https://claude-motion.com/skills/event-promo/install.md
- premium · Social · hyperframes · 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 · 10s
- Last checked: 2026-10-14

A bold poster layout: the event name rises from masks, a framed date block counts up, time and place fade in, and a tilted accent ticker scrolls your speakers before flipping to the ticket CTA.

Good fit: Conferences, meetups and webinars; Announcing speakers and dates.
Not for: Events without a fixed date; Recaps of past events.
Inputs: event, date, details, speakers, cta, theme (ink | paper | electric | noir | sand), fontDisplay (serif | sans | grotesk | mono).

Example prompt: Use the event-promo skill for our meetup: event "AI Builders | Night", date "Dec 3", details "18:30 · The Loft, Berlin", speakers "Ana Ruiz | Tom Hale | Mei Lin", cta "RSVP → aibuilders.berlin". 1:1 for Instagram.

Claude Motion prompt: Make a 10-second 16:9 event announcement for [who will watch, e.g. designers in the Bay Area], playing [where, e.g. LinkedIn and the event page]. Use only the event details below; don't change a date, place or name. Deep navy background with a faint grid.

The event name rises from masks in a heavy geometric sans, first word white, second word violet, with the time and place fading in beneath. On the right, a rounded outlined date block tilts in, showing the month in small violet monospace above a huge day number counting up. A slightly tilted violet ticker band slides up from the bottom, scrolling speaker names separated by ✦. End frame: the ticker fades to the ticket line.
